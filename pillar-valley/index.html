Pillar Valley support

Build a pair of spacecraft, fit their hex decks, and watch automatic 2v2 battles. The beta is distributed through Apple's TestFlight app. An invitation works once Apple has approved the beta and testing is open.

Playing the beta

Tap either ship in Launch Bay to change its equipment. Local practice works without an account. Battle Online uses Sign in with Apple, asks you to choose a pilot name, and matches your current pair with a saved opponent. Opponents labeled bot are computer-managed teams. There are no ads or purchases.

Get help or send feedback

Contact Ben Lirio using the email link below. Include your device model, iOS version, app build, what happened, and what you expected. TestFlight also offers Send Beta Feedback. Never send your Apple password, verification codes, or sign-in credentials.

Account and privacy

Settings includes Sign out and Delete account. Deleting an online account removes its cloud ships, rating, publications, sessions and associated battle recordings, and revokes this app's Sign in with Apple authorization. Your current ships remain saved locally on the device. If you cannot access deletion, contact support.

Pilot names and conduct

Do not use hateful, threatening, sexually explicit, or harassing pilot names. Report an offensive name through the pilot's menu on the leaderboard or contact support. Blocking hides the pilot from your leaderboard and prevents future matches between your accounts. A match already started may finish. Reports are reviewed by the operator; names can be removed and online access suspended. Contact support to appeal a moderation decision.
