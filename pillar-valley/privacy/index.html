Pillar Valley privacy policy

Pillar Valley is operated by Ben Lirio. This policy describes information handled by the iPhone game and its online service. Contact the operator through the support email link below.

Local play

The app saves your current ships, editing history, preferences and limited performance diagnostics on your device. Local practice does not require an account or an internet connection. App performance diagnostics are not automatically uploaded by the game.

Online play

Sign in with Apple supplies an app-specific Apple account identifier and authorization credentials. The game does not request your Apple name or email address. The service stores that identifier, encrypted authorization credentials, a chosen pilot name, saved ship designs, published teams, rating, results and battle recordings to provide online battles and account recovery. Session credentials are stored in the device Keychain. Your pilot name, published ships and rating are visible to other players; the leaderboard also shows wins and losses.

Safety and support

The service stores blocked pilot identifiers and submitted reports, including the reported name, reason and involved account identifiers, to prevent unwanted matches and review abuse. If you contact support, the operator receives the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. Support correspondence stays in the support inbox until deleted; you may request its deletion.

Service providers and purposes

Apple provides sign-in and TestFlight distribution. Amazon Web Services hosts the game service and encrypted storage in the United States (Northern Virginia). Requests expose network information such as an IP address to service infrastructure; short-lived hashed request counters help prevent abuse. Operational logs are used to diagnose failures. These providers process information to operate and secure their services under their applicable terms and privacy protections. The game includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK, does not sell personal data, and does not use data for cross-company advertising or tracking. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request data.

Retention

Account data is retained while the account exists. Battle recordings and safety reports are scheduled to expire after 30 days; older published snapshots after 31 days; online sessions after seven days. Automated storage removal can take additional processing time after expiry. Operational logs and database recovery backups retain seven days. A durable hashed deletion receipt is retained to prevent a backup restore from reviving a deleted account; it contains no ship design or Apple credential. Apple handles TestFlight feedback and diagnostics under its own privacy terms.

Your choices and deletion

You can play local practice without signing in, change your pilot name, block another pilot, or sign out in Settings. To remove your online account, choose Settings → Delete account and confirm. Deletion revokes this app's Apple authorization, removes cloud/private/published account data, sessions, related reports and shared recordings containing the account. Backup copies age out under the seven-day retention period; deletion receipts prevent them from restoring the deleted account. Local ships remain on your device. Contact support for help with access, correction, or deletion, including if you cannot open the app.

Permissions and changes

The game does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos, and has no purchases or push notifications. It is not offered in Apple's Kids Category. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date.
